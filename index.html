about

I first fell in love with photography by noticing the beauty of nature and landscapes, capturing the light and details often overlooked. Over the past year, I explored more of portrait and studio photography and developed a deeper interest in capturing peoples' personalities and the memories that matter to them.

I'm a student studying cognitive science at UC Berkeley, with a strong interest in how people experience the world around them. I teach the photography principles decal (class) where I get to share both the technical skills and the ways photography can be used to connect people together.
